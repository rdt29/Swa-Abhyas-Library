About the library

Built for deep, uninterrupted focus

Swa-Abhyas is a serene reading room designed around one job: helping students concentrate. Comfortable seating, steady lighting, and a house rule of silence make it a dependable place to put in the hours — every single day.

  • Dedicated silent study zones
  • Individual reading lamps at every seat
  • Clean, well-ventilated interiors
  • Open early to late, seven days a week
